See what the photo really costs

A free Chrome extension for AliExpress search results.

Add to Chrome – it's free

Many AliExpress listings show the price of their cheapest variant, like a bag of bullets, a refill or a single piece, next to a photo of the full product. PricePeel peels back the corner of each search result photo to reveal what the pictured item really costs.

  • Real price at a glance: an orange corner shows the price of the item in the photo when the listed price is for something else.
  • Hover for details: shipping, the total, and which variant the listed price was really for.
  • Private: runs only on AliExpress, in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. No tracking, ads or affiliate links.
  • Polite: checks only products you look at, caches results for a day, and stays within AliExpress's request limits.
