Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

PricePeel is a browser extension that shows the real price of the item pictured in AliExpress search results, when a listing advertises the price of a cheaper accessory variant. This policy explains what the extension does with data. In short: the PricePeel extension does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it doesn't send anything to its developer.

What the extension reads

PricePeel runs only on AliExpress pages (aliexpress.com and aliexpress.us). On search and listing pages it reads the product cards shown on the page: product IDs, advertised prices, thumbnails and labels such as "Choice" or "Bundle deals".

What the extension requests, and from whom

  • Product details from AliExpress. For each product card you look at, PricePeel asks AliExpress for that product's variants and prices, using the same request the AliExpress product page makes, in your own browser and with your existing AliExpress session. These requests go only to AliExpress.
  • Product images from AliExpress's image servers (aliexpress-media.com, alicdn.com), to compare a listing's thumbnail with its variant photos. The images are processed in memory and not stored.

The extension sends nothing to its developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or affiliate code, and it does not change AliExpress links.

What the extension stores, and where

Everything stays on your own device:

DataWhereHow long
Product variant and price data, to avoid looking the same product up repeatedly The extension's local storage in your browser24 hours, then deleted
Times of recent lookups (to stay under AliExpress's rate limit) and whether lookups are paused AliExpress's site storage in your browserLookup times: one minute; a pause: up to two hours
Whether you collapsed or closed PricePeel's "paused" note AliExpress's site storage in your browserUntil you clear site data

No account, name, email address, payment information, browsing history outside AliExpress, or location is collected or stored by the extension. Removing the extension deletes its local storage; clearing AliExpress's site data removes the rest.

This website and the contact form

This website has no analytics, ads or tracking cookies. If you send a message through the contact form, what you enter (your message, and optionally an AliExpress link and your email address) is stored by the site's host, Netlify, and is used only to read and answer your message. It isn't shared, sold or used for marketing. To have a message deleted, ask through the same form.

Children

PricePeel does not knowingly collect any information from children.
